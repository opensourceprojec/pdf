How to Convert a PDF Page to PNG — 4 Simple Steps

  1. Upload your PDF — Click or drag-and-drop your PDF file into the tool above.
  2. Select the page — Enter the page number or use the slider to navigate to any page.
  3. Choose resolution — Pick from Normal (72 DPI), High (150 DPI), Ultra (300 DPI), or Max (400 DPI).
  4. Download as PNG — Click the download button and your PNG image saves instantly to your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PDF Snap free to use?

Yes, PDF Snap is completely free with no account, no registration, and no usage limits.

How do I convert a PDF page to a PNG image?

Upload your PDF, enter the page number you want, then click "Download Page as PNG". The image saves directly to your device.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Your PDF never leaves your device. All conversion happens entirely inside your browser — there is no server involved.

What resolution PNG can I export?

You can export at Normal (72 DPI), High (150 DPI), Ultra (300 DPI), or Max (400 DPI). For print-quality images, choose Ultra or Max.

Does it work with password-protected PDFs?

Currently, password-protected PDFs are not supported. The tool works with standard, unencrypted PDF files.
